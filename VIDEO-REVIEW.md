# Video evidence and suitability review
**Prepared:** 11 November 2026 · **Status:** Curated external teaching examples; final viewing and approval pending

## Selection principles
The academy prioritises observable patient-centred counselling, motivational interviewing, OARS, teach-back and hypertension adherence. Preference is given to videos referenced by recognised public health agencies, professional organisations, university programmes or training manuals. A video used here is **not** a Jamaican clinical guideline. All external videos are optional and their embed ability has not been independently tested in WRHA Moodle.

## Why these were chosen
- **Medication adherence:** The CDC/Million Hearts videos give a grounded context for common barriers, team-based action and follow-up. US-specific rates and systems are not appropriate as Jamaica data.
- **Counselling demonstrations:** The motivational interviewing role plays allow CHAs to recognise open questions, reflections, empathy, and change talk. They are not models for prescribing.
- **Teach-back:** The Penobscot video shows ineffective and more effective explanations; the improved example begins at approximately **5:16** according to Health Literacy Solutions.
- **NCD relevance:** The diabetes/obesity motivational interviewing example is directly linked to NCD counselling and is more relevant than generic mental-health-only scenarios.
- **Pedagogical fit:** Every lesson includes a specific observation question; learners can discuss what worked and what they would do differently in a Jamaican community setting.

## Critical review requirements
1. **Open and watch every full video** (including its audio, captions and clinical examples) before final approval. The research verified trusted *references* and descriptions, but direct YouTube playback was not accessible from the research environment.
2. **Test each embedded video** in the target Moodle installation; creators can disable embedding or change availability. A direct YouTube link is always provided.
3. **Check accessibility**, captions, length and audio level; provide a text alternative or approved written learning activity where practical.
4. **Check clinical scope:** any practitioner-specific medical or medication decisions must not become CHA instructions. Jamaican MOHW 2024/2025 guidelines and WRHA clinical protocols control such instructions.
5. **Check rights:** Use YouTube's official player, never download/rehost videos without permission; if WRHA requires offline access, obtain the publisher's written approval.
6. **Check institutional privacy and policy:** External videos are loaded only after a click, using YouTube's privacy-enhanced embed domain; institutional rules may still block YouTube.

## Additional excellent external resources (not embedded by default)
- [USC Reboot Diabetes clinician training](https://sites.usc.edu/rebootdiabetes/person-centered-care/clinician-training/): A three-visit diabetes consultation sequence with open questions, affirmations, reflections, change talk and summaries. Verify the individual streaming sources before integrating.
- [University of Nebraska Medical Center MI demonstrations](https://www.unmc.edu/patient-safety/catch-rural-falls/motivational-interviewing/interviewing-education.html): Eight-minute motivational interviewing patient case.
- [USUHS Motivational Interviewing Resource Center](https://cdp.usuhs.edu/motivational-interviewing): A twenty-minute demonstration, coded and uncoded transcripts and supporting activities.
- [RISE implementation toolkit](https://www.rise4health.com/toolkit): Short OARS demonstrations and adherence-barrier client stories; HIV context, so apply communication lessons only.
- [Government of Canada behaviour-change scenarios](https://www.canada.ca/en/public-health/services/chronic-diseases/videos-on-supporting-behaviour-change.html): Motivational interviewing and 5 As, but its 5 As sequence is not identical to WRHA's proposed five-step framework.

**No lessons or certificates are gated by watching a video.**
