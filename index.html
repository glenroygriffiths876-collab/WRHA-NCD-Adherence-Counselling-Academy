START HERE

Welcome to your course

There are nine interactive modules arranged in three learning units. All lessons are available at any time. Each includes objectives, teaching content, Jamaican case studies, downloadable references and an optional knowledge check.

Assessment pathway: WRHA Moodle can provide a baseline pre-test before learning and a formally graded post-test after the lessons. These are separate from the practice checks in this prototype.

Practical workshop: Facilitator-assessed counselling skills remain a separate certification requirement. All nine online lessons are freely accessible regardless of progress.
