# Comprehensive teaching expansion — WRHA NCD Adherence Counselling Academy

**Development version:** 12 November 2026  
**Status:** Authoring implemented in prototype; **not** clinical endorsement, official LMS deployment or approval to issue certificates.

## What changed

- Replaced the nine brief learner-facing lesson bodies with **27 substantive guided lessons**, three within each of the existing nine modules. The original concise sections remain available in a collapsed quick-reference panel.
- Each guided lesson includes an explicit objective, three paragraphs of teaching, an original Jamaican application case, practical exercise, three-choice formative check with rationale, takeaway and reference note.
- Added **27 independent practice questions** across the lessons. Their answers are deliberately visible in browser source because they are formative only, not official examination questions.
- Added an **optional 18-question extended practice review** across the nine modules, with immediate rationales and a per-module summary. The original nine-question recap also remains.
- Added **10 downloadable blank training-tool templates**: 5 A's pocket guide, barrier-action worksheet, action plan, referral/handover, family support agreement, home-visit/peer-group guide, objective note, follow-up log, referral directory maintenance and three-month supportive supervision.
- Updated the displayed study-time estimate to approximately **910 minutes (15 hours 10 minutes)** including guided practice. This is an instructional design estimate, **not pilot-validated hours or credit**.
- Preserved the non-linear module navigation, current progress and resume logic, curated nine-video mapping, institutional logo, Ministry PDF resource library, original illustration system, current prototype certificate and 1.2 SCORM packaging.
- Extended the SCORM manifest and build workflow so all new assets are included and the number/shape of lessons, short checks, video mappings and tools are validated.

## Source reconciliation and limits

- Existing WRHA October 2026 proposal, participant manual and facilitator pack provided the nine-module baseline, local 5 A's (Ask, Assess, Advise, Agree, Assist), existing exercises, CHA role limits and supportive-supervision focus.
- The 49-page *WRHA NCD Academy Comprehensive Teaching Package* (12 Nov 2026) supplied original curriculum development content, 27-lesson map, NCD dialogues and tool designs. This repository uses newly worded learner-facing teaching, not an exact conversion of the PDF.
- The 13-page *WRHA NCD HSTU Curriculum Transfer Brief* (12 Nov 2026) supplied cautious observations and proposed themes around home visits, ethics, counselling, team practice, peer support and self-care.
- HSTU materials were **partially** reviewed with authorised learner access, not comprehensively audited. No protected HSTU slides, videos, assessments, handbook extracts or ART-specific instructions were reproduced here.
- Jamaican MOHW 2024 hypertension and diabetes and 2025 asthma guidance remain primary clinical reference points, subject to checking for newer approved institutional instructions.

## What remains before official rollout

1. **Clinical sign-off:** CHA scope, any reference to vital-sign thresholds, emergency directions, prompt-referral priorities, diabetes foot problems, hypoglycaemia teaching and medication-related explanations.
2. **Institutional sign-off:** approved privacy/contact SOPs, real referral pathways, verified local service directory, legitimate use of logo and material rights.
3. **Learning sign-off:** 40-point observed competency rubric, safety-critical failures, pass mark, remediation process, formal post-test, study-hour estimate and facilitator standards.
4. **Moodle:** upload to sandbox; test actual learner identities, SCORM grade/completion behaviour, mobile access, certificate restrictions and separate observed-practical grades. Browser-local prototype data is not official training evidence.
5. **Media:** human review of nine YouTube videos end-to-end, captions, embed availability, rights and module fit. A single source identification is not a playback audit.
6. **User experience:** full browser and mobile smoke test, screen-reader review, download behaviour and user pilot before production approval.

### Suggested practical competency rubric — **proposal only**

Ten domains at 0–4 points each (maximum 40): (1) opening/privacy, (2) agenda/listening, (3) empathy/reflections, (4) barrier assessment, (5) information within scope, (6) autonomy and motivation, (7) WRHA 5 A's, (8) agreement/teach-back, (9) referral and urgency, (10) objective documentation/follow-up.

Suggested anchors: 0 absent or unsafe; 1 major gaps; 2 partial with prompting; 3 independent appropriate performance; 4 consistently responsive. **No automatic pass score is endorsed.** Unauthorised medication changes, avoidable emergency delay, serious privacy breaches or discriminatory/coercive treatment should trigger supervised remediation regardless of numeric total. The existing facilitator pack's provisional 30/40 requires explicit training-team confirmation.

## Code governance

All live patient records, identifiable learning reflections, secured exam items, official marks and certificate credentials must remain inside approved institutional systems, not this public repository. The new templates do not save or transmit client data. Course completion in the current site remains **prototype practice status** rather than formal certification.
